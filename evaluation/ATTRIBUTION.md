`sample_demo.mp4` is a 960 × 540, 10 FPS, H.264 conversion of the full approximately 15.5-second source clip. The conversion removes audio and changes the resolution, frame rate, and container. This adapted demo clip and its manual count annotations are shared under CC BY-SA 3.0. The source author does not endorse QueueWatch.

The manual CSV labels one frame per second. Counts include visible pedestrians in the frame, including people in the far-side crossing area. These are hand-counted sample labels, not annotations supplied by the original author.
